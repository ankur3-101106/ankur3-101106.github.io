Unit - 10

TITLE

Blood Relations​

1. Introduction and Family Tree Framework​

Blood relation problems test logical reasoning by analyzing complex familial connections across successive ancestral and descendant generations.

1.1 Lineage Sides: Paternal vs. Maternal​

Familial relationships are fundamentally bifurcated based on the parental lineage:

Detailed Relationship Reference​

Relationship DescriptionFormal Kinship Designation

Father's mother / Mother's motherGrandmother (Paternal / Maternal)

Father's sister / Mother's sisterAunt (Paternal Aunt / Maternal Aunt)

To draw fast and unambiguous family tree diagrams:

[!IMPORTANT] Never assume the gender of a person merely from their first name (e.g., Suman, Kiran, Robin). Gender must be explicitly stated in the problem text or deduced through defined relationships (such as "mother", "father", "son-in-law", "wife").

2. Types of Blood Relation Problems​

2.1 Type 1: Pointing / Introducing Statements​

In these questions, a speaker describes an individual by detailing a chain of kinship backwards from themselves or an anchor entity.

  • Deconstruction Technique:
    1. Identify the reference pronoun: "My", "His", "Her".
    2. Parse the phrase from the end towards the beginning (Right-to-Left breakdown).
    3. Example: "He is the only son of the father of my grandfather."
      • "My grandfather"   ⟹  G+2\implies G+2⟹G+2
      • "Father of my grandfather"   ⟹  \implies⟹ Great-grandfather (G+3G+3G+3)
      • "Only son of my great-grandfather"   ⟹  \implies⟹ Grandfather (G+2G+2G+2)
      • "He is the son"   ⟹  \implies⟹ Father or Uncle (G+1G+1G+1).

2.2 Type 2: Coded Blood Relations​

Kinship links are represented by mathematical symbols or punctuation:

  • E.g., P+QP + QP+Q means "PPP is the father of QQQ"; P−QP - QP−Q means "PPP is the sister of QQQ"; P×QP \times QP×Q means "PPP is the brother of QQQ".

Fast Elimination Techniques​

  1. Gender Filter: If the question asks "How is MMM related to TTT as an uncle?", MMM must be male. Any answer choice where MMM is coded as female or where MMM's gender cannot be determined can be immediately eliminated.
  2. Generation Gap Matrix (ΔG\Delta GΔG):
    • Assign numerical values: Father/Mother =+1= +1=+1, Brother/Sister =0= 0=0, Son/Daughter =−1= -1=−1.
    • Sum the generation steps across the expression.
    • If asking for a Grandfather relation, the net sum must equal +2+2+2. Any option yielding a different net generation gap is invalid.

2.3 Type 3: Family Tree Puzzles​

Complex multi-sentence scenarios involving 6 to 10 family members with overlapping attributes (generations, marital pairs, occupations, hobbies).

  • Solution Strategy:
    1. Tabulate the fixed married couples first.
    2. Map vertical generational branches.
    3. Ensure count constraints (e.g., "only two married couples", "three generations", "two male children") match the tree.

3. Solved Problems and Exam Practice​

Problem 1 (Pointing to a Photograph)​

Pointing to a photograph of a boy, Suresh said, "He is the son of the only son of my mother." How is Suresh related to that boy?

Solution:

  1. "My mother"   ⟹  \implies⟹ Suresh's mother.
  2. "The only son of my mother"   ⟹  \implies⟹ Suresh himself (assuming Suresh is male, being an only son).
  3. "He is the son of [Suresh]"   ⟹  \implies⟹ Suresh's son. Therefore, Suresh is the father of the boy.

Problem 2 (Introducing a Lady)​

Pointing to a lady, a man said, "The son of her only brother is the brother of my wife." How is the lady related to the man?

Solution:

  1. "My wife's brother"   ⟹  \implies⟹ the man's brother-in-law.
  2. "The son of her only brother is [the brother of my wife]".
  3. This means the lady's brother is the father of the man's wife.
  4. The father of one's wife is father-in-law.
  5. The lady is the sister of the father-in-law. Therefore, the lady is the sister of father-in-law (or paternal aunt of the man's wife).

Problem 3 (Coded Blood Relation - Gender & Gap)​

Read the following definitions:

  • A+BA + BA+B means AAA is the brother of BBB
  • A−BA - BA−B means AAA is the sister of BBB
  • A×BA \times BA×B means AAA is the father of BBB Which of the following means that CCC is the son of MMM?
  1. M−N×C+FM - N \times C + FM−N×C+F
  2. F−C+N×MF - C + N \times MF−C+N×M
  3. N+M−F×CN + M - F \times CN+M−F×C
  4. M×N−C+FM \times N - C + FM×N−C+F

Solution: CCC must be male (CCC is a son), and generation gap between MMM and CCC must be M→C=+1M \to C = +1M→C=+1.

  • In Option 1:
    • N×C  ⟹  NN \times C \implies NN×C⟹N is father of CCC.
    • M−N  ⟹  MM - N \implies MM−N⟹M is sister of NNN.
    • Thus MMM is aunt of CCC. Incorrect.
  • In Option 4:
    • Let us test M×C+FM \times C + FM×C+F: if M×CM \times CM×C, MMM is father of CCC.
    • Let us re-examine: CCC must be male. In C+FC + FC+F, CCC is brother of FFF (male).
    • If the expression is M×C+FM \times C + FM×C+F: MMM is father of CCC, and CCC is male   ⟹  C\implies C⟹C is the son of MMM. Therefore, an expression with M (parent)…C (male)M \text{ (parent)} \dots C \text{ (male)}M (parent)…C (male) satisfies the condition.

Problem 4 (Family Tree Puzzle - 8 Members, 3 Generations)​

Study the information carefully and answer the questions:

  • Eight persons are in three generations of a family with two married couples.
  • PPP is the son-in-law of HHH, who has three children.
  • MMM is the nephew of LLL.
  • WWW is the father of KKK, who is the sister-in-law of UUU.
  • UUU is the only daughter of HHH, who is the father of NNN.
  • LLL is unmarried.
  • NNN and KKK don't have any children.

Questions:

  1. How is PPP related to MMM?
  2. How is KKK related to NNN?
  3. How many male members are in the family?

Solution:

  1. Generations and Couples Analysis:
    • HHH has three children and is in Generation 1.
    • UUU is the only daughter of H  ⟹  UH \implies UH⟹U is female.
    • Since HHH has three children, and UUU is the only daughter, the other two children must be sons.
    • HHH is father of N  ⟹  NN \implies NN⟹N is a son (male).
    • LLL is unmarried, so LLL must be the other son (male).
    • Thus, children of HHH are: U[−]U [-]U[−], N[+]N [+]N[+], L[+]L [+]L[+].
  2. Couples:
    • PPP is son-in-law of HHH. Since UUU is the only daughter, PPP must be married to UUU: P[+]=U[−]P [+] = U [-]P[+]=U[−]
    • KKK is sister-in-law of UUU, and NNN is married to KKK: N[+]=K[−]N [+] = K [-]N[+]=K[−]
    • WWW is father of K  ⟹  W[+]K \implies W [+]K⟹W[+].
  3. Children in Generation 3:
    • NNN and KKK have no children.
    • LLL is unmarried.
    • MMM is nephew of L  ⟹  ML \implies ML⟹M must be the son of PPP and UUU: M[+] is the son of P and UM [+] \text{ is the son of } P \text{ and } UM[+] is the son of P and U
  4. Answering the questions:
    • Q1: PPP is the father of MMM.
    • Q2: KKK is the wife of NNN.
    • Q3: Count of male members: H[+]H [+]H[+], P[+]P [+]P[+], N[+]N [+]N[+], L[+]L [+]L[+], W[+]W [+]W[+], M[+]  ⟹  M [+] \impliesM[+]⟹ 6 males (assuming HHH is father; if HHH's gender is grandfather, exactly 5 or 6 depending on whether HHH or WWW is specified).

Problem 5 (Profession and Relationship Puzzle)​

In a family of six members P,Q,R,S,T,UP, Q, R, S, T, UP,Q,R,S,T,U, there are two married couples. The members include a doctor, a lawyer, a teacher, an engineer, a nurse, and a student:

  • RRR, a lawyer, is married to the teacher.
  • TTT, an engineer, is the son of RRR and brother of UUU.
  • SSS, a doctor, is the grandmother of UUU.
  • PPP, a housewife/nurse, is the mother of TTT. How is UUU related to SSS?

Solution:

  1. Generation +2+2+2: Grandmother SSS (doctor, female).
  2. Married couples:
    • Couple 1 (Gen +1+1+1): RRR (lawyer, male) is married to PPP (female, teacher/housewife).
    • SSS is grandmother   ⟹  S\implies S⟹S is in Gen +2+2+2.
  3. Generation 000: TTT (engineer, son, male) and UUU (sibling of TTT).
  4. Since SSS is the grandmother of UUU, UUU is the grandchild (grandson or granddaughter) of SSS. If UUU's gender is female, UUU is the granddaughter; if unspecified, grandchild.

Problem 6 (Point-to-Point Chain)​

Pointing to Ketan, Namrata said, "He is the son of my father's only son." How is Ketan's mother related to Namrata?

Solution:

  1. "My father's only son"   ⟹  \implies⟹ Namrata's brother.
  2. "He is the son of [Namrata's brother]"   ⟹  \implies⟹ Ketan is Namrata's nephew.
  3. Ketan's mother is the wife of Namrata's brother. Therefore, Ketan's mother is Namrata's sister-in-law.

Problem 7 (Sibling Direct Statement)​

AAA is the brother of BBB. BBB is the daughter of CCC. DDD is the father of AAA. How is CCC related to DDD?

Solution:

  1. AAA and BBB are siblings (AAA is male, BBB is female).
  2. BBB is daughter of CCC, and AAA is son of DDD.
  3. Therefore, CCC and DDD are the parents of AAA and BBB.
  4. Since DDD is the father (male), CCC must be the mother (female) and the wife of DDD.
